Value frame · Not a price list

A system replaces expensive alternatives.

Executives do not buy infrastructure off a menu. They choose between hiring, buying enterprise software, or delaying growth. We frame the decision against what you would spend without us.

Alternative A

In-house senior engineer

$110k–$150k+/yr

US SaaS developer salary averages about $110k. Fully loaded true cost pushes past $75/hr before overhead — before product, design, QA, or ops.

Alternative B

Enterprise SaaS / custom build

Six figures+

An in-house build typically runs 40–60% more than an equivalent agency engagement once you count time, tools, and the months of delay.

Alternative C

Do nothing

$150k+ in lost leverage

Missed bookings, broken handoffs, and manual ops compound. The cost of delay is usually larger than the cost of the stack.

Systems engagements start at $25k. Scoped after discovery — never from a slider.
