Systems and Automation

The Backend Is the Business

By Cody Cochran · September 24, 2026 · 5 min read

The shoot is the front of the house. People post the front. The business is everything that has to be true before the camera comes out and after the card is backed up: the contract, the deposit, the folder, the review, the invoice, the archive. Creators who only practice the front stay talented and disorganized. The backend is the business.

The path a paid job should walk

Write it down until it is boring. A lead becomes a one-page offer. The offer becomes a contract. A deposit holds the date. A folder exists before call time, with names a collaborator can understand. The shoot happens. The edit has a review link, not a text thread of stills. Approval triggers delivery and the invoice. Payment triggers the archive. If any of those steps live only in your head, the business is a mood.

Cochran Films built the studio on that sequence. The essay on workflow automation is the hour-by-hour version. You do not need custom software on day one. You need the sequence to exist when you are tired.

Creator Collective is the money path

Creator Collective is where that sequence is a product, not a pep talk. Members get the job board, the community, the courses, and 500 or more assets. Jobs can move through a clearer path: listing, application, contract, and payment, with escrow-aware workflows so you are not sending finals and hoping. Collective Member is $37 a month, with a 4 percent platform fee on marketplace and job payouts. Stripe processing still applies, about 2.9 percent plus 30 cents, and the fee is on the job total.

Collective Pro is $60 a month. On eligible payouts the fair-use line is 0 percent on the first $200 in a month, then 2 percent. Pro adds client invoicing, unlimited daily asset downloads, strategy sessions, and the right to run your own paid community. On that community, Creator Collective takes 20 percent capped at $4 a member. The point of Pro is simple: if you are already getting paid, keep more of it, and stop rebuilding invoices in a blank document. The full comparison is on the membership page. Cody and JB talk through the money on the Creator Collective channel, including the episode embedded in the profit essay.

Bizzi Cloud is the file path

Money without files is a promise. Files without a system are a favor. Bizzi Cloud is the backend for the media itself. Create the project folder when the deposit hits. Review in that project. Deliver with a branded transfer or a gallery. Invoice from a transfer link when the plan includes it. Paid plans include Gallery Suite. Pro, at $30 a month for 2 TB, adds team sharing so an editor can work without your password. AES-256 at rest and in transit is the baseline, not an upsell.

If the job only works when you remember every step, you do not have a business. You have a good memory and a calendar.

What to install this week

  • One offer page, one contract, one deposit rule.
  • A folder template you duplicate per client. Put it in Bizzi Cloud.
  • A review link and a delivery link that are not the same vague email.
  • An invoice that goes out when the work is approved, through Creator Collective if you are on Pro.
  • A note, the day you are paid, that splits job cost, tax, pay, and reinvestment.

A direct next step

Draw the last job as a list of steps and circle the one you did from memory. Replace that circle with a template before the next inquiry. Join Creator Collective for the money path and open Bizzi Cloud for the files. The front of the house can stay beautiful. The backend is what lets you do it again on purpose.
