A Bench, Not a Payroll

By Cody Cochran · September 23, 2026 · 5 min read

A bigger job tempts a creator to hire an employee. Sometimes that is the right pain. Most of the time it is early. A salary, taxes, benefits, gear, and a quiet month you still have to fund will break a studio that only needed a second shooter on Thursday. Keep a bench. Add a seat. Call a person. Skip the payroll until the calendar can carry it.

What a bench actually is

A bench is a short list of people who are good at the gap in your offer, who answer, and who can see the files without your password. A second shooter. An editor. A gaffer. A photographer when you are on the video. A DJ when the job is a room that also needs a floor. You do not employ them. You book them, you pay them clearly, and you work with them again because the last job was clean.

Creator Collective is built for that list. The community is working videographers, photographers, and editors who refer overflow instead of pretending to do every role. The marketplace is where members list services and gear. The job board is where paid work is posted for people already inside the standard. Membership starts at $37 a month. You still have to be someone worth calling. The room does not do the rep for you. The essay on wanting the win more than they do applies to collaborators too.

Share the folder, not the login

A bench falls apart in access. If the only way to collaborate is your personal drive and a password in a text, you do not have a team. You have a liability. Bizzi Cloud prices seats in the open. Bizzi Pro is $30 a month for 2 TB and up to 3 seats at $10 each, with team sharing and permissions. Bizzi Network is $55 for 5 TB and up to 8 seats. We Bizzi is $100 for 10 TB and up to 10 seats. The collaborator sees the project. You keep the account. Gallery Suite, review, and branded transfers stay on the same plan, so the client still receives one finished handoff.

That is the operator version of a lesson clients already hear in in-house versus an agency. Headcount is the expensive way to buy a skill you need twice a month. A seat plus a trusted person is the version that survives January.

Hire the role for the job in front of you. Hire the salary when the calendar has already paid for it three months running.

How to build the list without a casting call

  • Name the three gaps that made the last big job stressful.
  • Meet those skills inside Creator Collective and book a small paid test, not a vague coffee.
  • Put the test project in Bizzi Cloud and add a seat. Judge the person by the folder, the communication, and the file names.
  • Pay on a written number. A bench that works "for exposure" will not be there when the date matters.
  • Keep two names per gap. One person is a single point of failure.

DJs who need a shooter, or shooters who need a DJ, can cross the same family of platforms. DJs Need Luv 2 is the DJ home. Creator Collective is the wider creator room. Introduce people on purpose. A referral you can trust is worth more than a cold post.

Watch how Cody and JB talk about partnerships on Creator Collective. The standard is mutual. If someone will not do the small job cleanly, they will not do the large one cleanly either.

A direct next step

Write the three names you would call this week if a second body were required. If the list is blank, join Creator Collective and fill it with people you have paid, not people you have followed. Give them a Bizzi Cloud seat on the next job. Payroll can wait. The bench cannot.
