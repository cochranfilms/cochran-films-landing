Founder and CEO

Cody Cochran did not set out to chase a job. He set out to build something that keeps working after the shoot wraps. The cameras were the vehicle. The destination is a network of creators, businesses, and platforms.

He is the founder of Cochran Films, an Atlanta and Douglasville studio for video, photography, podcasts, events, web, and the systems behind them. He is also a partner and co-owner of Creator Collective, where he helps media entrepreneurs build production skills, clearer offers, and businesses that can repeat. The same practice shows up in Course Creator Academy, Bizzi Cloud, and DJs Need Luv 2.

Creators deserve better. Not just better cameras. Better systems. Better opportunities. Better businesses.

The work spans brand films, civic stages, and the unglamorous pipelines that get a file to a client intact. That includes rooms like the Mayor Andre Dickens Inaugural Ball instant-print experience, conference coverage, and the operating notes in this journal. Learn, earn, connect, build. That is the order.
